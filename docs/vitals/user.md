# Progress and lab orders

Open **Health** to view your readings and weight goal. Use **View lab orders** to see lab order statuses, collection methods and recorded dates from your connected medical record.

Lab history is read-only. It does not include lab results or interpretation, and a results-received date is not a link to results. Missing dates say **Not recorded**. Use **Previous page** and **Next page** to browse.

If your medical record is not connected, use **Connect your record**. An empty list means no lab orders are available in this connected record; a temporary service failure has its own message. If asked, sign in again or complete two-step verification.
