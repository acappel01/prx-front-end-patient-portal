# Orders from this site

Open **Record → View orders from this site** to see orders connected to your account. Use **Previous page** and **Next page** to browse; open **View order** for items, recorded amounts and shipment tracking.

Your clinical order history remains on **Record**. The histories may overlap and are shown separately. An empty site history does not mean your clinical provider has no orders; purchases that have not been connected to your account will not appear here.

Order totals show the amount recorded for each order in its currency. A pending order or displayed total does not confirm payment collection. This view does not charge, refund or retry a purchase.

If your session expires, sign in again. If asked to set up two-step verification, complete it to continue. A temporary service failure is shown separately from an empty history.
